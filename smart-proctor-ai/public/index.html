Multi-Layered Defense Architecture

How SmartProctor AI Protects Exam Integrity

👤

1. Head Pose & Face Tracking

Monitors 2D/3D facial landmarks and boundary centroid displacement. Triggers instant violation alerts when a candidate looks away left/right, casts gaze downwards suspiciously, leaves their seat (missing face), or brings a secondary collaborator into the frame.

📱

2. Unauthorized Object Detection

Embedded real-time Computer Vision (COCO-SSD / MobileNet) detects contraband objects in camera view, including cell phones, smart tablets, headphones, and reference books. Automatically timestamps and snapshots evidence frames.

🖥️

3. Background App & OS Audit

Combines browser sandbox lockdown (Page Visibility API, window blur, fullscreen enforcement, clipboard blocking) with a lightweight companion process agent that monitors native OS tasks for Discord, Telegram, WhatsApp, AnyDesk, and screen capture tools.
